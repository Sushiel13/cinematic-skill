# Maatrubhumi: May War Rest in Peace (fan-made tribute): credits and licences

This is an unofficial, fan-made tribute video. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Salman Khan Films,
the film's makers or its cast. It contains no footage, stills, posters, music or artwork from the film.
A label on every frame says so: "FAN-MADE TRIBUTE · NOT OFFICIAL FOOTAGE".

## Visuals
Every frame is drawn in code (Skia, via the cinematic-skill engine, MIT licence): the mountain ridge, smoke,
explosions, soldier and helicopter silhouettes, convoy, tactical map, photo frame, letter, flag and all type.
The soldiers, vehicles and map are symbolic. They show no real person, unit, place or event.
The Indian flag is drawn to the official proportions (3:2, Ashoka Chakra with 24 spokes).

No photographs are used.

## Narration (Hindi voice-over)
- Synthesised with Piper (rhasspy/piper, MIT licence), voice `hi_IN-pratham-medium`
  (fallbacks `hi_IN-rohan-medium`, `hi_IN-priyamvada-medium`), from huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices.
  Check the voice's MODEL_CARD there for the training data's licence before commercial use.
- Pitched down and slowed slightly in post (ffmpeg).

## Music and sound
- Score: original, composed in code for this film (`make_score.py`): military drums, bass and strings,
  all synthesised with numpy/scipy. No samples, nothing licensed.
- Sound effects: synthesised by cinematic-skill's `build_sfx.py`. No samples.
